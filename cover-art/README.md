# v4.9.3 character cover assets

The 101 original character illustrations are stored as 480×720 WebP images for the Android and web cover studio. The set contains 40 male covers, 30 female covers, and 31 couple or family covers. The images were created for this app and do not reproduce a specific work, character, or published cover.

The gallery adds mood and genre filters and includes distinct designs for plus-size adults, Joseon court settings and hanbok, naval and military uniforms, special operations and spies, doctors, and preschool-age children in family scenes. Titles and author names are composited at preview and save time.
